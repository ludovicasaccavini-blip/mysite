The next operating system is one interface that reshapes itself around your task.

Today one piece of work takes four tabs: a model, a doc, a browser and a chat. The next shift puts one surface in front of you that understands what you are doing, takes the shape the task needs, and runs the apps quietly underneath.

Apple won the PC by betting on design, not specs. AI makes the same bet possible again. Apps become the back end, the browser sits underneath, and the interface itself becomes the product. Local models make it fast and private enough to change shape in real time. The winner owns a library of beautifully designed modes and the judgment to pick the right one before you ask.

What could prove me wrong

Anti thesis

People love muscle memory. Studies of adaptive menus found users felt disoriented and preferred static ones. And the shell is the giants' turf: Google already ships generative UI in Gemini and Search, and OpenAI is building its own device.

Kill signal

Apple or Google ships task shaped modes inside the OS, and startups in the space end as acquihires, like Dreamer to Meta, instead of standalone companies.

Where a startup wins anyway

Pick one profession, own its modes and its data, and run locally so firms trust it with confidential work. Start where switching hurts most, such as an investor moving between model, memo and deck, then expand.
