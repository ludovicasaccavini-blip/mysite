Every company will need secure hosting for its agents.

Agents are leaving employees' laptops and starting to work in teams. The winners host them securely, set house rules for how they talk to each other, and prove they are safe.

Class prototype

As swarms turn whole jobs into products and their owners struggle to control cost, safety and risk, the conversation between agents becomes the new control point. We back operators who write its rulebook.

Rewritten

Agents are leaving laptops and starting to work in teams. We back the platforms that host them securely, let colleagues share the best ones, and set the house rules for how they talk, with proof a CISO or insurer can sign.

Plain words
Agent
Software that does a task on its own, like an assistant that drafts or files things.
Swarm
A team of agents. A lead agent splits the job and hands pieces to helpers.
A2A
Agent to agent. Agents messaging each other, like an email thread between bots.
Kill switch
An off button for agents. Now a standard feature.
The thesis in three layers

Hosting, house rules, proof

Each layer answers one question a CISO asks before saying yes. The giants own the basics; the open space is in the middle and on top.

Hosting · where they run

Run agents on company servers, not laptops, in one library where colleagues copy what works.

Why it matters

  • OpenClaw alone has 135,000+ running copies, mostly self hosted
  • About 60% of AI risks come from tools non developers built

Who is there

  • AWS, Microsoft, Google, OpenAI, Anthropic
  • Open: neutral hosting across vendors (Credal, Dust)

House rules · how they talk

Who may talk to whom, when to ask a human, what tone to use, built from how the company really works.

Why it matters

  • 1,200 test agents built their own chat channel; 700 joined a breach
  • Off buttons stop agents; nothing steers them

Who is there

  • Celonis Context Model, Microsoft Guardian Agents
  • Open: steering agent to agent work (Trace, Onyx)

Proof · are they safe

Practice runs before launch, a record of every step, insurance if something breaks.

Why it matters

  • Standard policies exclude AI since January 2026
  • JPMorgan blocks outside agents until they prove themselves

Who is there

  • Tracing tools, mostly acquired
  • Open: rehearsal and insurance (Arga Labs, AIUC)

Submission 2, the three lines

What could prove me wrong

Anti thesis

This is the giants' turf. AWS, Microsoft, Google, OpenAI and Anthropic all host agents, and OpenAI already lets teams share them. Will big companies want neutral hosting, or just pick one vendor?

Kill signal

Most large enterprises pick one vendor to host all their agents, that vendor ships house rules for agent to agent work, and neutral players like Dust and Credal stall before their next round.

Alternatives that lost

  • Kill switches: now a feature
  • Orchestration frameworks: labs give them away
  • AI spend control: Stripe and Ramp moved first
  • Agent identity: 8+ acquisitions in a year
  • Governance dashboards: suites own them
  • Raw sandbox compute: sells to labs, not enterprises
