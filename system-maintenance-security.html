System maintenance and security

AI made finding bugs almost free. Fixing still runs on people. PHP2Shell showed what that costs: a 2024 finding nobody fixed, a volunteer building old packages, governments offline.

Why it is big and burning

Finding bugs is now almost free, and fixing them is not. Anthropic's Glasswing project found more than 10,000 flaws in one month and 97 were patched. PHP2Shell, a bug sitting in PHP since 2012, was found with AI and reached about 70% of the web. Most of the world's software cannot be patched quickly, and every gap is a live door for attackers. That is why the budgets are moving now.

Five hypotheses

What held up, and what changed

SupportedH1

Finding bugs is no longer the bottleneck

Starting hypothesis: AI made discovery fast; the gap is fixing.

  • Of 1,596 bugs Anthropic's discovery reported to open source projects, 97 were fixed when CSA checked.
  • NIST parked about 29,000 backlogged CVEs as Not Scheduled in April 2026.
  • Finders are richly funded: XBOW at $1B+, depthfirst at $580M post, RunSybil $40M.

Now. Kept. Discovery is where the money went first, which is exactly why the fix side is underbuilt.

SupportedH2

The choke point is upstream maintenance

Starting hypothesis: Volunteer maintainers cannot absorb AI scale reports.

  • A PHP core developer found part of the bug in a 2024 audit; the team could not agree on a fix, so it waited.
  • The PHP Foundation had 13 staff; one volunteer built packages for old versions.
  • The PHP security team funded in 2026 is one engineer in residence for six months.

Now. Kept, with a twist: this is funded by grants today. A company can win here only by selling to the people downstream who need the fix.

PartlyH3

You cannot fix what you cannot see

Starting hypothesis: Inventory is the first gap.

  • 59% of PHP hosts that show a version run a branch with no fix; 740,000 hide their version.
  • PHP inside firewalls and control panels was attacked too.
  • But ServiceNow paid $7.75B for Armis and Tenable bought Vulcan: big platforms already own inventory.

Now. Narrowed: the open gap is inventory inside appliances and vendor software, not inventory in general.

SupportedH4

Shields buy time, not safety

Starting hypothesis: Virtual patching matters but is not a fix.

  • Cloudflare had rules live about 9 hours before disclosure, and attackers changed payloads within hours.
  • A third flaw came out two days after the patch, fixable only by a config change.
  • Oligo raised $60M on runtime blocking, with revenue up 300% in a year.

Now. Kept. Shields are the bridge; the business sits on either side of them, in the fix and in proof the fix is safe.

CorrectedH5

Insurance will force patch speed

Starting hypothesis: Carriers will refuse claims for unpatched systems.

  • Early losses came from precautionary shutdowns, not breaches (Marsh Re).
  • Coalition says it has no clause blocking cover for skipped patches.
  • Modelers disagree on how systemic this is: CyberCube calls it not a 1 in 200 year event.

Now. Corrected: insurers will price patch speed rather than exclude it. They need data on it first, which is the opening.

Where I would look first

Five white spaces

Where the player map is thin and the business model is clear.

Moving fastest

Backports for the long tail

AI written, tested fixes for retired branches of PHP and other runtimes, sold as support contracts.

59% of exposed PHP hosts run unfixed branches; CRA makes old code a compliance issue; HeroDevs raised $125M.

Zone: Backports & old versions
Moving fastest

Patch proof in minutes

Rehearse a patch on a copy of the real app and its old dependencies, then certify it safe to ship.

Five months to fix complex apps once a patch is ready; only 9% patch in 24 hours.

Zone: Patch testing & rollout
Shifting

Maintainer triage copilot

Verify, dedupe and draft fixes for AI reports before they reach a small volunteer team.

97 of 1,596 Glasswing bugs fixed; PHP has one engineer in residence for ecosystem security.

Zone: Triage & disclosure
Shifting

Inventory inside appliances

Find PHP and other runtimes inside firewalls, routers and control panels that web scanners skip.

Attacks on firewall management interfaces; old cPanel PHP branches got no fix.

Zone: Exposure & inventory
Shifting

Patch speed score for insurers

Measured time to patch per company, sold to carriers and brokers who now need it.

Shutdowns drove early losses; carriers treat skipped patches differently.

Zone: Cyber insurance
